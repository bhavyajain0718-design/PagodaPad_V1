How it works

From idea to a live trading pair in four steps.

1

Connect

Link MetaMask or Rabby. The site adds Robinhood Chain for you.

2

Create

Choose a token name, symbol and supply.

3

Pair

Pick a Chinese stock to trade against and add ETH as seed liquidity.

4

Trade

Your pool goes live and its volume counts toward that stock’s leaderboard.

Why pair with stocks?

Most launches sit in a lonely ETH pool. Pairing with a recognisable equity gives a token a stronger anchor and a ready audience.

A credible counterpart

A token quoted against BABA or PDD has a clearer reference price than a bare ETH pair.

Volume you can see

Every trade rolls up per stock, so you can tell which pairs attract real activity.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is this live on mainnet?

No. Today it is an interface prototype with simulated data. Contracts are not deployed yet.

Which chain does it use?

Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum Layer 2 built on Arbitrum. Testnet chain ID is 46630 and mainnet is 4663.

Are the stocks real shares?

They would be tokenized representations traded on-chain. Which Chinese stocks are available as tokens on Robinhood Chain still needs to be confirmed.

Is this financial advice?

No. Launching and trading tokens is risky, and you can lose everything you put in.
