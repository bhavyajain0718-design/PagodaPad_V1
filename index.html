FAQ

Quick answers.

Is this live on mainnet?

No. It is an interface prototype with simulated data. Contracts are not deployed yet.

Which chain does it use?

Robinhood Chain, an Ethereum Layer 2 built on Arbitrum. Testnet chain ID is 46630 and mainnet is 4663.

Are the stocks real shares?

They would be tokenized representations traded on-chain. Which Chinese stocks exist as tokens on Robinhood Chain still needs to be confirmed.

Wallet connect does not work on my phone

Normal mobile browsers have no wallet. Open this page inside the MetaMask or Rabby in-app browser.

Is this financial advice?

No. Launching and trading tokens is risky and you can lose everything you put in.
